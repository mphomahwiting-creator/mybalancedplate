Our Story

My Balanced Plate started from a simple interest in preparing meals and understanding what goes into the food we eat.
Through preparing different meals, photographing the finished plates and exploring their ingredients and portions,
the idea developed into a vision for a business that could help people better understand and enjoy the food they eat.
As part of this process, we use Journable as a supporting tool to help analyse meals and estimate portions, calories and macronutrients.
This allows us to present our meals with additional information that customers can use to better understand what's on their plate.

What started as an interest in preparing and understanding meals is developing into My Balanced Plate,
a future healthy-food business focused on prepared meals and practical meal-preparation guidance.
