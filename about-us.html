Our Story

My Balanced Plate started from a simple interest in preparing meals and understanding what goes into the food we eat.

Through preparing different meals, photographing the finished plates and exploring their ingredients and portions, the idea developed into a vision for a business that could help people better understand and enjoy the food they eat.

As part of this process, we use Journable as a supporting tool to help analyse meals and estimate portions, calories and macronutrients. This allows us to present our meals with additional information that customers can use to better understand what's on their plate.

From Interest to Vision

What started as an interest in preparing and understanding meals is developing into My Balanced Plate, a future healthy-food business focused on prepared meals and practical meal-preparation guidance.

WHAT WE OFFER

Our Value Proposition

At My Balanced Plate, we make healthy eating simple, practical and enjoyable.

We provide thoughtfully prepared meals with a focus on balanced ingredients and appropriate portions, while helping you understand what is on your plate.

Our meals are supported by Journable, which we use to estimate food components, portions, calories and macronutrients. This information is presented in a simple way to help you make more informed choices about your meals.

Good food. Clear information. Better choices.

OUR PROCESS

How We Do It

From Preparation to Plate

We carefully prepare our meals using a variety of ingredients and thoughtful portions.

We then photograph each meal and use Journable as a supporting tool to help us understand the food components and estimate portions and nutritional information.

We review the results and present the information in a simple, easy-to-understand format, so you can see what is included in your meal and make more informed choices.
